PJECT CONTEXT What is BakeSync ERP? Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
System specifically designed for bakery operations Cloud-based platform
that integrates all aspects of bakery management Multi-tenant
architecture supporting multiple businesses on a single platform
Role-based access control for Owners, Bakers, and Cashiers Core Purpose:
Streamline bakery operations through digital transformation, enabling
medium to semi-large bakeries to scale efficiently while maintaining
quality and profitability.

Traditional bakery operations face significant challenges: Manual
Processes: Time-consuming inventory counting, recipe management, and
sales recording Data Fragmentation: Information scattered across
notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory Error-Prone Operations: High risk of
calculation errors, inventory discrepancies, and production mistakes
Limited Visibility: Lack of real-time insights into business performance
Scalability Issues: Difficulty managing growth and multiple locations
Impact: Reduced efficiency, increased costs, and limited business growth
potential PBLEM STATEMENT

PROBLEM STATEMENT Market Gap Identified Generic POS Systems: Don't
address bakery- specific needs (recipes, production, ingredient
tracking) Enterprise ERP Systems: Too complex and expensive for medium
to semi-large bakeries Spreadsheet Solutions: Limited functionality,
prone to errors, not scalable Target Market Medium to Semi-Large
Bakeries (15-50 employees) Multi-Location Bakery Chains Cafes and

OBJECTIVE OF THE STUDY Solves Real-World Problems - Addresses specific
challenges faced by medium to semi- large bakery operations Improves
Operational Efficiency - Reduces manual work and errors through
automation Provides Business Insights - Enables data-driven
decision-making through analytics Scales with Business Growth - Supports
businesses from single location to multi-location operationsTo develop a
comprehensive ERP system that: Automate Manual Processes - Eliminate
manual inventory counting, automate recipe scaling, streamline sales
processing Improve Data Accuracy - Reduce human errors, provide
real-time synchronization, implement validation Enhance
Decision-Making - Provide real-time metrics, generate comprehensive
reports, enable predictive insights Support Multi-User Collaboration -
Implement role-based access, enable team collaboration, track user
activitiesSpecific Objectives

Inventory Management - Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts,
expiration tracking, bulk operations Recipe Management - Recipe
database, ingredient scaling, cost calculation, production history
Production Logging - Batch tracking, automatic ingredient deduction,
production history, cost tracking Point of Sale (POS) - Transaction
processing, multiple payment methods (cash/card/GCash), receipt
generation, automatic inventory updates Financial Management - Sales
tracking, expense management, financial reports, profitability analysis
Team Management - Role-based access, email invitations, activity
tracking, permission management Supply Chain - Supplier management,
purchase order creation and tracking, delivery management Web-based
application (desktop, tablet, mobile), cloud-hosted database, modern web
technologies (Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase), comprehensive testing

CONCSION BakeSync ERP addresses real-world challenges faced by medium
to semi-large bakeries by solving critical problems like manual
inventory counting, recipe scaling errors, and lack of real- time
business insights that cost bakeries thousands monthly. Built with a
modern technology stack using Next.js, TypeScript, and Supabase, the
system provides a robust, secure, and performant cloud-based solution
specifically designed for bakery operations with role-based access
tailored for owners, bakers, and cashiers.
