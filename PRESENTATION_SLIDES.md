## Slide 2: Project Context - Overview

### **What is BakeSync ERP?**

- **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System** specifically designed for bakery operations
  - *A comprehensive software solution that manages all bakery business processes in one integrated platform, eliminating the need for multiple disconnected systems.*

- **Cloud-based platform** that integrates all aspects of bakery management
  - *Accessible from any device with internet connection, allowing bakery managers to monitor operations remotely and team members to access information from anywhere.*

- **Multi-tenant architecture** supporting multiple businesses on a single platform
  - *Multiple bakery businesses can use the same system while maintaining complete data isolation and security, making it cost-effective for bakery chains.*

- **Role-based access control** for Owners, Bakers, and Cashiers
  - *Different user roles have access to different features based on their responsibilities, ensuring data security and operational efficiency.*

**Core Purpose**: Streamline bakery operations through digital transformation, enabling medium to semi-large bakeries to scale efficiently while maintaining quality and profitability.

## Slide 3: Project Context - Problem Statement

### **The Challenge for Medium to Semi-Large Bakeries**

**Traditional bakery operations face significant challenges:**

- **Manual Processes**: Time-consuming inventory counting, recipe management, and sales recording
  - *Real-life situation: A medium-sized bakery with 15 employees spends 2-3 hours daily manually counting inventory across multiple storage areas, leading to discrepancies and production delays.*

- **Data Fragmentation**: Information scattered across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory
  - *Real-life situation: Production manager needs to check three different notebooks and a spreadsheet to determine if they have enough ingredients for tomorrow's batch, wasting 15-20 minutes per production planning session.*

- **Error-Prone Operations**: High risk of calculation errors, inventory discrepancies, and production mistakes
  - *Real-life situation: A baker miscalculates recipe scaling for a large order, resulting in wasted ingredients worth $200 and delayed order fulfillment, damaging customer relationships.*

- **Limited Visibility**: Lack of real-time insights into business performance
  - *Real-life situation: Bakery owner cannot determine daily profitability until end-of-month manual calculations, missing opportunities to adjust pricing or reduce costs in real-time.*

- **Scalability Issues**: Difficulty managing growth and multiple locations
  - *Real-life situation: A semi-large bakery chain opens a second location but struggles to coordinate inventory, recipes, and sales data between locations, leading to inconsistent operations.*

**Impact**: Reduced efficiency, increased costs, and limited business growth potential for medium to semi-large bakeries.

## Slide 4: Project Context - Industry Need & Target Market

### **Why Bakery-Specific ERP?**

**Market Gap Identified:**

- **Generic POS Systems**: Don't address bakery-specific needs (recipes, production, ingredient tracking)
  - *Generic systems lack recipe scaling, production batch tracking, and ingredient consumption features that bakeries require for efficient operations.*

- **Enterprise ERP Systems**: Too complex and expensive for medium to semi-large bakeries
  - *Large enterprise systems like SAP or Oracle are designed for massive corporations, costing $50,000+ annually, which is unaffordable for most medium-sized bakeries.*

- **Spreadsheet Solutions**: Limited functionality, prone to errors, not scalable
  - *Excel-based systems break down when multiple employees need simultaneous access, leading to version conflicts and data loss.*

**Target Market:**
- **Medium to Semi-Large Bakeries** (15-50 employees)
  - *Bakeries with established operations, multiple team members in different roles (owners, bakers, cashiers), and sufficient scale to benefit from ERP automation.*

- **Multi-Location Bakery Chains**
  - *Bakery businesses operating 2-5 locations that need centralized management and coordination.*

- **Cafes and Restaurants with Significant Bakery Operations**
  - *Food service establishments with dedicated bakery teams and production facilities requiring specialized management.*

**Real-Life Application**: A medium-sized bakery with 20 employees (1 owner, 8 bakers, 6 cashiers, 3 managers, 2 support staff) uses BakeSync to coordinate production schedules, manage inventory across multiple storage areas, track sales from three POS terminals, and generate financial reports for decision-making.

## Slide 5: Project Context - Solution Overview

### **BakeSync ERP Solution**

**Integrated Modules:**

1. **Inventory Management** - Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, expiration tracking
   - *Applied in ERP: When a baker logs production, ingredients are automatically deducted from inventory. If flour drops below the 50kg threshold, the owner receives an immediate alert on their dashboard, preventing stockouts during peak production periods.*

2. **Recipe Management** - Recipe database with cost analysis and scaling
   - *Applied in ERP: A production manager needs to scale a cookie recipe from 24 to 100 cookies. The system automatically calculates all ingredient quantities, shows cost per batch ($12.50), and displays profit margin (65%), enabling quick pricing decisions.*

3. **Production Planning** - Batch tracking with automatic inventory updates
   - *Applied in ERP: A baker logs a batch of 50 croissants. The system automatically deducts 2.5kg flour, 1kg butter, and other ingredients from inventory, adds 50 croissants to finished goods stock, and records production cost for profitability analysis.*

4. **Point of Sale (POS)** - Fast transaction processing with multiple payment methods
   - *Applied in ERP: During lunch rush, a cashier processes 60 transactions per hour using the POS system. Each sale automatically updates inventory, records payment method (cash/card/GCash), and generates receipts, eliminating manual entry errors.*

5. **Financial Management** - Sales tracking, expense management, profitability analysis
   - *Applied in ERP: Owner views dashboard showing today's sales ($1,250), expenses ($450), and profit ($800) in real-time. System generates monthly reports comparing product profitability, helping identify that croissants have 70% margin vs. 45% for bread, guiding production decisions.*

6. **Team Management** - Role-based access, invitations, activity tracking
   - *Applied in ERP: Owner invites a new baker via email. Baker receives invitation, creates account, and immediately has access to recipes and production logging but cannot view financial data or manage suppliers, ensuring appropriate data security.*

7. **Supply Chain** - Supplier management and purchase order tracking
   - *Applied in ERP: Manager creates purchase order for flour. System tracks order status, sends alerts when delivered, automatically updates inventory upon receipt, and records expense in financial module, streamlining procurement workflow.*

## Slide 7: Objective of the Study - Technical & Business Objectives

### **Technical Objectives**

1. **Architecture Design** - Implement scalable multi-tenant architecture, ensure data security and isolation, optimize for performance
   - *Applied in ERP: System uses Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring each bakery's data is completely isolated. A bakery owner can only see their own inventory, recipes, and sales data, even though multiple bakeries use the same database infrastructure.*

2. **User Experience** - Create intuitive interface, design role-specific dashboards, ensure mobile responsiveness
   - *Applied in ERP: Baker dashboard shows production schedule and recipe access, while owner dashboard displays financial metrics and team management. Cashier interface focuses on POS functionality, each optimized for specific role needs.*

3. **System Integration** - Integrate all modules seamlessly, provide real-time synchronization, enable automated workflows
   - *Applied in ERP: When a sale is processed in POS, inventory automatically updates, financial records are created, and if stock drops below threshold, owner receives notification - all happening in real-time without manual intervention.*

### **Business Objectives**

1. **Cost Reduction** - Reduce labor costs through automation, minimize waste through better inventory management, decrease error-related costs
   - *Real-life impact: Saves 4+ hours daily on manual processes across 20 employees, equivalent to $3,450 monthly in labor cost savings for a medium-sized bakery.*

2. **Revenue Growth** - Increase transaction capacity, reduce stockouts, enable data-driven pricing
   - *Real-life impact: POS system processes 50% more transactions per hour, and inventory alerts prevent stockouts that previously caused $400-2,000 monthly in lost sales.*

3. **Operational Excellence** - Improve process efficiency, enhance team productivity, enable scalable operations
   - *Real-life impact: Production efficiency improves by 33-50%, with bakers completing 3-4 batches per hour instead of 2-3, increasing daily output capacity.*

## Slide 8: Scope and Limitation - Project Scope

### **In-Scope Features**

**Core Business Modules:**

1. **Inventory Management** - Real-time stock tracking, low-stock alerts, expiration tracking, bulk operations
   - *Applied in ERP: System tracks all raw materials (flour, sugar, butter) and finished goods (bread, pastries) with automatic updates from production and sales. Manager receives alerts when flour drops below 50kg threshold, preventing production delays.*

2. **Recipe Management** - Recipe database, ingredient scaling, cost calculation, production history
   - *Applied in ERP: Production manager accesses recipe database, scales cookie recipe from 24 to 100 units with automatic ingredient calculations, views cost per batch ($12.50) and profit margin (65%), enabling informed pricing decisions.*

3. **Production Logging** - Batch tracking, automatic ingredient deduction, production history, cost tracking
   - *Applied in ERP: Baker logs production of 50 croissants. System automatically deducts ingredients from inventory, adds finished goods to stock, records production time and cost, and updates dashboard metrics in real-time.*

4. **Point of Sale (POS)** - Transaction processing, multiple payment methods (cash/card/GCash), receipt generation, automatic inventory updates
   - *Applied in ERP: Cashier processes sale of 3 croissants and 2 coffees. System calculates total ($15.50), processes GCash payment, generates receipt, automatically deducts items from inventory, and records sale in financial module.*

5. **Financial Management** - Sales tracking, expense management, financial reports, profitability analysis
   - *Applied in ERP: Owner views dashboard showing today's sales ($1,250), expenses ($450), profit ($800), and product-level profitability. System generates monthly reports comparing performance across products and time periods.*

6. **Team Management** - Role-based access, email invitations, activity tracking, permission management
   - *Applied in ERP: Owner invites new baker via email. Baker receives invitation link, creates account, and gains access to recipes and production logging but cannot view financial data, ensuring appropriate security based on role.*

7. **Supply Chain** - Supplier management, purchase order creation and tracking, delivery management
   - *Applied in ERP: Manager creates purchase order for 100kg flour from Supplier ABC. System tracks order status, sends delivery alerts, automatically updates inventory upon receipt, and records expense in financial module.*

**Technical Scope**: Web-based application (desktop, tablet, mobile), cloud-hosted database, modern web technologies (Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase), comprehensive testing suite.

## Slide 9: Scope and Limitation - Limitations

### **Out of Scope Features**

1. **Mobile Native Applications** - Native iOS/Android apps (web-responsive design only), push notifications, offline mode
   - *Rationale: Web-responsive design provides cross-platform compatibility without requiring separate app development and maintenance, reducing complexity and cost.*

2. **Advanced Integrations** - Third-party accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero), payment gateway integrations, e-commerce platforms, barcode scanner hardware
   - *Rationale: Focus on core bakery operations first. Integrations can be added post-launch based on user feedback and demand. Real-life impact: Bakery wanting QuickBooks integration must wait for future enhancement, requiring manual data export/import.*

3. **Advanced Features** - AI-powered demand forecasting, automated ordering systems, customer loyalty programs, marketing campaign management
   - *Rationale: These features require significant additional development and may not be essential for initial launch. Can be considered for future enhancements.*

### **Technical Limitations**

1. **Platform Limitations** - Web-based only (requires internet connection), browser compatibility (modern browsers), no offline functionality
   - *Impact: Users need reliable internet connection. System cannot function during internet outages, though data syncs automatically when connection is restored.*

2. **Performance Limitations** - Dependent on internet speed, cloud service provider limitations, scalability subject to plan limits
   - *Impact: Slow internet connections may affect system responsiveness. System performance scales with Supabase plan tier, requiring plan upgrades for very large operations (100+ employees).*

3. **Integration Limitations** - Limited third-party integrations, no hardware integration (barcode scanners, receipt printers), no direct payment processing
   - *Impact: Manual data entry required for some processes. Payment processing requires manual entry of amounts, though system tracks payment methods.*

### **Functional Limitations**

1. **Feature Limitations** - Basic reporting (no advanced analytics), limited customization options, no workflow automation builder, basic notification system
   - *Impact: Users cannot create custom automated workflows. Reporting provides essential metrics but lacks advanced predictive analytics or machine learning insights.*

2. **User Limitations** - Maximum users per business (subject to plan), limited role types (Owner/Baker/Cashier only), basic permission system
   - *Impact: Very large bakeries with 100+ employees may need custom role configurations. Current system supports standard bakery organizational structures (15-50 employees).*

3. **Data Limitations** - Limited export formats (CSV, basic PDF), no bulk import capabilities, limited data migration tools
   - *Impact: Initial data entry requires manual input. Large historical data migration would need custom scripts or manual entry.*

### **Key Assumptions**

- **User Assumptions**: Basic computer literacy, reliable internet connection, understanding of bakery operations terminology, modern web browsers
- **Business Assumptions**: Medium to semi-large operations (15-50 employees), standard bakery operations, sufficient scale for ERP benefits
- **Technical Assumptions**: Cloud infrastructure availability, database performance meets requirements, browser compatibility

**Real-Life Constraint**: System assumes bakery has at least 15 employees with distinct roles. A small bakery with only 3 employees might find the system too complex, while a very large bakery with 100+ employees might need additional customization.

## Slide 10: Real-Life Application - Complete Workflow Example

### **BakeSync ERP in Action: A Day in a Medium-Sized Bakery**

**Scenario**: "Sweet Dreams Bakery" - A medium-sized bakery with 20 employees (1 owner, 8 bakers, 6 cashiers, 3 managers, 2 support staff) operating from 6 AM to 8 PM.

**Morning (6:00 AM - Production Planning):**
- **Owner** logs into dashboard, sees low-stock alert for flour (45kg remaining, threshold: 50kg)
  - *Applied in ERP: Inventory Management module sends automatic alert. Owner creates purchase order in Supply Chain module, which will automatically update inventory when delivered.*

- **Production Manager** checks production schedule, selects "Chocolate Chip Cookies" recipe, scales from 24 to 100 cookies
  - *Applied in ERP: Recipe Management module automatically calculates ingredient requirements (8.3kg flour, 2.5kg sugar, etc.), shows cost per batch ($12.50), and profit margin (65%).*

**Mid-Morning (8:00 AM - Production):**
- **Baker** logs production of 100 cookies, system automatically deducts ingredients from inventory
  - *Applied in ERP: Production Logging module deducts ingredients, adds 100 cookies to finished goods inventory, records production cost, and updates dashboard metrics in real-time.*

- **Inventory** now shows flour at 36.7kg, triggering another low-stock alert
  - *Applied in ERP: Inventory Management module continuously monitors stock levels and sends alerts when thresholds are crossed, preventing stockouts.*

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - Sales Peak):**
- **Cashier** processes 60 transactions per hour using POS system
  - *Applied in ERP: POS module processes sales quickly, automatically updates inventory (deducts sold items), records payment methods, generates receipts, and creates sales records in Financial Management module.*

- **Owner** views real-time dashboard showing $1,250 in sales, $450 in expenses, $800 profit
  - *Applied in ERP: Financial Management module aggregates all sales and expenses in real-time, providing instant business performance visibility without manual calculations.*

**Evening (6:00 PM - End of Day):**
- **Owner** generates daily sales report, identifies that cookies generated highest profit margin
  - *Applied in ERP: Financial Management module generates comprehensive reports showing product-level profitability, enabling data-driven production planning for next day.*

**Result**: Bakery operates efficiently with automated processes, real-time visibility, and data-driven decision-making, saving 4+ hours daily and preventing costly errors.
