## Executive Summary

**BakeSync ERP** is a comprehensive, cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning system specifically designed for medium to semi-large bakeries, cafes, and food service businesses with established operations and team structures (15-50 employees). Built with modern technology and a deep understanding of bakery operations, BakeSync streamlines every aspect of your business—from inventory management and recipe tracking to point-of-sale transactions and financial analytics.

### Core Value Proposition

BakeSync ERP eliminates the chaos of manual processes, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. Our platform provides a unified solution that integrates inventory management, production planning, sales processing, and financial tracking into one intuitive system. With real-time data synchronization, role-based access control, and mobile-friendly design, BakeSync empowers your team to work efficiently while giving you complete visibility into your business operations.

### Target Market

BakeSync ERP is designed for:
- **Medium to Semi-Large Bakeries** - Established bakeries with 15-50 employees managing complex recipes and production schedules, requiring coordinated team operations and role-based workflows
- **Multi-Location Bakery Chains** - Bakery businesses operating 2-5 locations that need centralized management and coordinated operations across sites
- **Cafes and Restaurants with Significant Bakery Operations** - Food service establishments with dedicated bakery teams (15+ employees) and production facilities requiring specialized management

**Why Medium to Semi-Large?** - These bakeries have established operations with multiple team members in distinct roles (owners, bakers, cashiers, managers), sufficient scale to benefit from ERP automation, and the organizational structure to effectively utilize role-based access control and team management features. Small bakeries with fewer than 15 employees or single-person operations typically don't have the team structure required for the system's multi-user, role-based design.

## Problem Statement

### The Challenges of Manual Bakery Operations

Running a bakery involves managing countless moving parts—ingredients, recipes, production schedules, inventory levels, sales transactions, and team coordination. Without proper systems, these operations become overwhelming, leading to:

#### **Inventory Management Nightmares**
- Manual stock counting takes hours and is prone to errors
- Stockouts occur unexpectedly, disrupting production
- Expired ingredients go unnoticed, leading to waste
- No visibility into ingredient costs and consumption patterns
- Difficulty tracking inventory across multiple locations

#### **Recipe and Production Inefficiencies**
- Recipes stored in notebooks or scattered digital files
- No standardized scaling for batch sizes
- Difficulty tracking which recipes are most profitable
- Manual calculation of ingredient requirements
- No production history or batch tracking

#### **Sales and Financial Blind Spots**
- Manual transaction recording is slow and error-prone
- No real-time visibility into daily sales performance
- Difficulty reconciling cash and card transactions
- Limited insights into best-selling products
- Time-consuming financial reporting

#### **Team Coordination Challenges**
- Unclear role assignments and permissions
- Difficulty tracking who did what and when
- Manual communication about inventory needs
- No centralized system for team collaboration

#### **The Cost of Manual Processes**

Manual processes don't just slow you down—they cost money:
- **Time Loss**: 2-3 hours daily on manual inventory counting and data entry
- **Error Costs**: Inventory discrepancies and production mistakes
- **Waste**: Expired ingredients and overproduction due to poor visibility
- **Lost Sales**: Stockouts preventing sales during peak hours
- **Labor Costs**: Inefficient workflows requiring more staff time

**The Solution**: BakeSync ERP automates these processes, saving time, reducing errors, and providing the insights you need to grow your business profitably.

## System Architecture

### Multi-Tenant Architecture

BakeSync ERP uses a sophisticated multi-tenant architecture that allows multiple businesses to operate on the same platform while maintaining complete data isolation. Each business has its own secure workspace with:

- **Unique Business Identifier**: Each business gets a custom URL (e.g., `yourshop.bakesync.com`)
- **Data Isolation**: Complete separation of data between businesses
- **Shared Infrastructure**: Efficient resource utilization while maintaining security

**Benefits**:
- Lower costs through shared infrastructure
- Automatic updates and improvements for all users
- Scalable architecture that grows with your business

### Role-Based Access Control

BakeSync implements granular role-based access control with three primary roles:

#### **Owner**
- Full system access
- Financial reports and analytics
- Team management
- Supplier and purchase order management
- System configuration

#### **Baker**
- Recipe management
- Inventory viewing and updates
- Production logging
- Dashboard access
- Cannot access financial data or POS

#### **Cashier**
- Point of Sale (POS) access
- Inventory viewing (for stock availability)
- Sales transaction processing
- Cannot access recipes, production, or financial data

**Security Features**:
- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users only see data they're authorized to access
- Automatic permission enforcement at the database level
- Audit trails track all user activities

### Cloud-Based, Accessible Anywhere

BakeSync ERP is built on modern cloud infrastructure, providing:

- **24/7 Availability**: Access your system anytime, anywhere
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Automatic Backups**: Your data is automatically backed up and secured
- **No Installation**: No software to install or maintain
- **Automatic Updates**: New features and improvements are delivered automatically

### Security and Data Protection

**Enterprise-Grade Security**:
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit and at rest

- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions with Row-Level Security
- **Data Isolation**: Complete separation between businesses
